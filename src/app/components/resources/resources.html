One Platform, Three Ways to Grow

📣

Marketing

Reach more customers. Drive more sales.

  • 👥
    Bulk Campaigns — send templated WhatsApp messages to your full list or a segmented audience, with optional media and scheduling.
  • 💻
    API Campaigns — trigger messages directly from your own systems or storefront events.
  • 🖼️
    Media Campaigns — share images, videos, and PDFs, including free media within a customer's active engagement window.
  • 🛒
    Abandoned Cart Recovery — Vbot automatically detects abandoned carts and sends WhatsApp reminders, tracking pending, converted, and recovered revenue.
  • 📄
    Templates — manage WhatsApp-approved templates across Marketing, Utility, and Authentication categories, with live approval status and instant preview.
💬

Support

Inbox that works like your team does.

  • 📥
    One Inbox, Every Conversation. All customer messages land in a single, unified inbox — no more switching between tools to find a conversation.
  • 🔍
    Smart Filters. Instantly filter by Unread, Inquiry, Cart, Order, Invoice, Sale, or Shipped — so your team sees exactly what needs attention first.
  • 🗂️
    Context Alongside Every Chat. See order status, cart contents, and customer tags right next to the conversation — no more asking customers to repeat themselves.
  • ↕️
    Always in the Right Order. Vbot keeps your inbox sorted by real activity — when a customer replies, that conversation moves back to the top, exactly where your team expects it.
  • 📤
    Reply From Anywhere. Text, media, quick replies, and template messages — all from the same screen, on desktop or mobile.
📊

Sales

Leads CRM that helps you close more deals.

  • 📊
    Visual Pipeline. Track every lead through a clear funnel — New, Hot, Warm, Cold, Converted, Lost.
  • ⏱️
    SLA Timers. Every lead gets a response-time countdown. Vbot flags leads nearing their SLA window with a warning before they're missed, and marks any that slip past as exceeded.
  • 👥
    Round-Robin Assignment. New leads are automatically cycled through your agent list in order — no lead sits unassigned, and no single agent gets overloaded.
  • 📋
    WhatsApp Forms. Build no-code forms for lead capture and sign-ups, with auto-trigger rules and instant preview before you publish.
  • 🛍️
    Shopify-Synced Leads. Orders and checkout activity from your store feed directly into your lead and contact base.
