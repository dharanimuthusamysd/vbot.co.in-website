About Vbot

Helping D2C brands turn WhatsApp into their biggest sales channel.

Our Story

Vbot was built for one reason: businesses were already having every important conversation with their customers on WhatsApp — orders, complaints, delivery updates, questions — but had no system behind it. Just a phone, a chat window, and a team trying to keep up.

We set out to give growing D2C and ecommerce brands a real backend for that conversation: a shared inbox, a leads pipeline, Shopify sync, and campaign tools — all built around WhatsApp instead of bolted onto it.

What We Believe

  • Customers shouldn't have to repeat themselves across five different tools.
  • Every message is a chance to either lose a sale or save one.
  • Support and sales are the same conversation — they shouldn't live in different apps.

Who We Serve

Vbot is built specifically for D2C and ecommerce businesses selling through Shopify, who want their WhatsApp presence to be as organized and trackable as the rest of their stack.
